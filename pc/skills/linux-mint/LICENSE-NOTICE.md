# Where this pack comes from

English rewrites, trimmed to Linux Mint (apt, Cinnamon, systemd, X11 and
Wayland sessions), of five skills from
[khalilbenaz/claude-skills-collection](https://github.com/khalilbenaz/claude-skills-collection)
(MIT licence, © Khalil Benaz): `systemd-manager`, `bash-scripting-expert`,
`linux-troubleshooter`, `linux-admin-guide` and `linux-security-hardener`.
The structure, the decision tables and the anti-pattern lists are theirs;
the wording, the Mint specifics and the trigger words are this repository's.
MIT permits this with the notice kept: it is kept here.
